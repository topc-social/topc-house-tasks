# TOPC House Neki ledger

TOPC House's project ledger: Neki earned for work on TOPC House. A person's share of TOPC House = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md. Tasks: the issues in this repo. Work done on topc-tasks before this repo existed stays in that repo's ledger.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at today's market price, priced from TOPC's rate card (`ecosystem/RATE_CARD.md` in the topc repo). Work from 2025 on uses the with-AI prices. Rows are only ever added, never edited.
